## 💬 Community Questions & Discussions

If you have questions about how to architect a layout, integrate `@alcspower/ui` with your state manager, or customize tokens:

👉 **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/AlcsPower/alcs-ui/discussions)**

- **Q&A:** Ask questions and get answers from maintainers and other developers.
- **Show & Tell:** Share projects and dashboards you've built with `@alcspower/ui`.
- **Ideas:** Share suggestions for new components, patterns, or presets.

---

## 🐛 Bug Reports & Technical Issues

If you've encountered an unexpected error, a broken component behavior, or an accessibility issue:

1. Search existing [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/AlcsPower/alcs-ui/issues) to avoid duplicate reports.
2. If your issue is new, open a bug report using our structured template:
   👉 **[Open a Bug Report](https://github.com/AlcsPower/alcs-ui/issues/new?template=bug_report.md)**

Please include a minimal reproduction snippet or repository.

---

## 🔒 Security Vulnerabilities

Please **do not** open public issues for security vulnerabilities. Report them confidentially via:
👉 **[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/AlcsPower/alcs-ui/security/advisories/new)**

Review our complete [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) for expected patch windows and CVSS classification.
